Session 4 — Preparation

Session 4

Before class (instructor)

  • [ ] Pilot Part A → B in a beaker: record I (tap) vs I (Na₂SO₄) at fixed V
  • [ ] Pilot Faraday arithmetic with those currents (students will copy your method)
  • [ ] Pilot U-tube — confirm 2:1 ratio visible within session time
  • [ ] Rehearse H₂ balloon demo (cathode-only feed, small balloon) or cancel per venue rules
  • [ ] Check graphite rods, stoppers, spade connectors, multimeters
  • [ ] Mix ~0.1 M Na₂SO₄ stock; label "NO SALT"
  • [ ] Pre-weigh Na₂SO₄ portions (~3.5 g) for beaker additions if helpful
  • [ ] Stage rusty nails, washing soda, sacrificial anodes, jars for Part E
  • [ ] Confirm overnight power policy for derusting cells

Day-of setup

  • [ ] Beaker stations: graphite, leads, series ammeter ready
  • [ ] U-tube stations filled or ready to fill with Na₂SO₄
  • [ ] Large sign: "No salt in electrolysis — use sodium sulfate"
  • [ ] Balloon demo area marked; extinguisher present; long lighter staged
  • [ ] Part E station ready for last 10 minutes

Common failure modes (from pilot)

Issue Prevention
Part A “broken” complaint Brief beforehand: tiny current is the point
Ammeter reads zero after Na₂SO₄ Wrong range; meter not in series; bad clip
Students grab salt Pre-brief; hide NaCl; labeled Na₂SO₄ only
Balloon mixes gases Dedicated cathode tube; anode vents free
Overpressure in U-tube Side arms open
