# Teaching Log

Fill this in **while the session is still fresh** — ideally in the ten minutes after the students leave, not the following week. Next year's version of you is the audience, and by then you will remember that something went wrong but not what.

The single most valuable entries are numbers: currents, voltages, minutes. Everything else you will roughly recall; the numbers you will not.
