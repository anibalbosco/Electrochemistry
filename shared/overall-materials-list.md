# Overall Materials List

Master checklist for the full week. Per-session quantities are in each session's `materials.md`.

## Buy-ahead list — the things that stop a session dead

| Item | Needed for | Why it is on this list |
|------|------------|------------------------|
| **Mains DC supply or 5 V USB bricks** | Sessions 4 and 5 | The overnight derusting cells will not survive on 9 V alkalines. This is the most common cause of a failed Session 5 |
| **Sodium sulfate (Na₂SO₄)** | Session 4 | The only permitted electrolysis electrolyte. Check whether yours is anhydrous or decahydrate |
| **Distilled water** | Session 3 (essential), Session 4 (ladder) | Tap chloride ruins the silver bath |
| **Silver nitrate** | Session 3 | Often needs ordering well ahead. The bath is now **0.05 M** — five times the old strength, so budget accordingly |
| **Potassium nitrate (KNO₃)** | Session 3 | Supporting electrolyte for the silver bath. Must be **chloride-free** — test it against AgNO₃ before mixing a batch. NaNO₃ works identically |
| **1 kΩ resistors** | Sessions 2 and 3 | The resistor sets the plating current. Without one, every bench gets a different answer |
| **Rusty iron** | Sessions 4 and 5 | Two matched nails per group. Rusty hardware is surprisingly hard to find on demand — start collecting early, or leave nails in saltwater for a fortnight |
| **Wooden splints** | Session 4 | The reliable gas identification |
| **Spare rubber stoppers** | Session 4 | A split stopper ends the U-tube run |
| **Spare multimeter fuses** | All week | A blown mA fuse reads zero and looks exactly like "no reaction" |

### Session 3 silver bath — 0.05 M AgNO₃ in 0.2 M KNO₃

| Batch | AgNO₃ | KNO₃ | Or NaNO₃ |
|-------|-------|------|----------|
| 250 mL | 2.12 g | 5.05 g | 4.25 g |
| 500 mL | 4.25 g | 10.11 g | 8.50 g |

Distilled water only. Dissolve the nitrate salt first. Half-strength silver option: 0.025 M AgNO₃ (1.06 g / 250 mL) with the same KNO₃ and half the current.

**Chloride-test the nitrate salt first.** A few drops of your dissolved KNO₃ into a few drops of AgNO₃ — clear is good, milky white means contamination and a ruined batch.

### Sodium sulfate — check which one you bought

| Batch | Anhydrous (142.04 g/mol) | Decahydrate (322.19 g/mol) |
|-------|--------------------------|----------------------------|
| 250 mL of 0.1 M | 3.6 g | 8.1 g |
| 500 mL | 7.1 g | 16.1 g |
| 1.0 L | 14.2 g | 32.2 g |

Decahydrate is more than half water by mass. Weighing it as if it were anhydrous gives a bath at under half the intended strength — which looks like a mysteriously feeble Session 4.

## Water guidance (tap vs distilled)

| Use | Water | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Session 1 fruit cells | Tap | Fruit juice dominates |
| Session 2, 0.5 M CuSO₄ | Tap acceptable | Very hard water may cloud the bath; plating still works |
| Session 3, silver bath and rinses | **Distilled** | Tap Cl⁻ precipitates AgCl and spoils the bath |
| Session 4 conductivity ladder, rung 1 | **Distilled** | The whole point of the rung |
| Session 4, 0.1 M Na₂SO₄ | Tap | The electrolyte overwhelms tap minerals |
| Session 5 saltwater jars | Tap | You are adding 5–10% NaCl anyway |
| Derusting baths | Tap | High ionic strength |

**Rule of thumb:** one jug of distilled for silver and for the ladder's first rung; tap for everything else.

## Optional / worth adding next year

| Item | What it buys |
|------|--------------|
| **Universal indicator** | Session 4: cathode turns blue-purple, anode red-yellow. Makes the half-reactions directly visible. Best cheap upgrade to the week |
| **Ferroxyl indicator** (K₃[Fe(CN)₆] + phenolphthalein) | Session 5: anodic sites blue, cathodic pink, in minutes rather than days. Would free Session 5 from depending on jars started on Day 1. Never acidify or heat it |
| **Silver anode** (sterling wire or a bullion round) | Session 3: replenishes Ag⁺, eliminates bath depletion and pH drift entirely. A one-off reusable purchase |
| Magnetic stirrer | Session 3: stirring roughly doubles the current the bath can sustain before it goes dendritic |
| Conductivity meter | Turns the Session 4 ladder quantitative, in µS/cm |
| Small solar panel | Session 4 energy discussion |
| pH meter | Alternative to indicator paper |

## Safety reminders

- No eating experimental materials
- **No NaCl in any electrolysis or derusting bath** — Session 5 corrosion jars only
- Silver nitrate: gloves and goggles; **never add ammonia to it** — ammoniacal silver forms explosive solids on standing
- Hydrogen ignition: instructor only, small volumes, never a sealed container
- Overnight cells: open jars, ventilated room
- Collect copper and silver waste; do not pour down the drain
- Ammeters in series; avoid shorting batteries
